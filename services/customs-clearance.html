UK Customs Clearance Coordination

Clear documentation and coordinated import steps for cargo arriving in the United Kingdom.

Request a shipment quoteEmail Oakview

Customs clearance requires accurate shipment information and compliance with the rules that apply to the goods. Oakview Freight Services helps businesses coordinate the information and logistics needed for UK import clearance, with the specific process confirmed for each shipment.

Prepare accurate shipment information

Commercial invoices, packing lists, transport documents, commodity codes, customs values and origin details may be required. Additional permits or certificates can apply to controlled goods.

Commodity classification and customs value

Correct commodity classification and customs valuation are important for import declarations and duty calculations. Classification and valuation should be checked against the product and transaction details.

Arrival, release and onward movement

Customs release, port or terminal handling and onward delivery are separate operational steps. We can discuss how these fit into your shipment plan and confirm responsibilities before dispatch.

Frequently asked questions

What documents are commonly required?

Common documents include a commercial invoice, packing list and transport document. Product-specific certificates, licences or other records may also be required.

Can customs clearance be guaranteed before shipping?

No. Clearance depends on accurate information, applicable controls, customs processing and any examination or document requests.

Who pays import duty and VAT?

Responsibility depends on the agreed commercial and import arrangement. Duties and taxes are assessed under applicable rules and should be accounted for separately from service fees.

Planning an India–UK shipment?

Send your cargo description, dimensions, weight, origin, UK destination and preferred timeframe. Oakview Freight Services will review the requirements and discuss suitable options.
