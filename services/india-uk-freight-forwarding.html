India–UK Freight & Logistics

India–UK Freight Forwarding

A coordinated freight partner for businesses moving cargo between India and the United Kingdom.

Request a shipment quoteEmail Oakview

Oakview Freight Services helps exporters, manufacturers and businesses plan cross-border cargo movements from India to the UK. We coordinate shipment requirements across air and sea freight options and help connect origin-side planning with UK import and delivery arrangements.

Freight planning around your cargo

The right shipping plan depends on cargo type, dimensions, weight, urgency, origin, destination and import requirements. Share these details with our team so the available routing and service options can be reviewed.

Air and sea freight options

Air freight may suit time-sensitive or smaller consignments. Sea freight may suit larger or less urgent cargo. Service selection, routing, transit estimates and pricing are confirmed against the actual shipment.

UK import coordination

Where required, we can discuss UK Importer of Record support, customs clearance coordination and onward delivery as part of the shipment plan. Import requirements depend on the goods and the importer’s circumstances.

Frequently asked questions

What information is needed for a freight quote?

Please provide the cargo description, number of packages, dimensions, gross weight, pickup city in India, UK destination and preferred shipping timeframe.

Can you arrange both air and sea freight?

We can review air and sea freight options for India–UK shipments and confirm the suitable service after assessing the cargo and route.

Can UK import support be included?

UK import and customs support can be discussed as part of the quote, subject to the goods, documentation and applicable requirements.

Planning an India–UK shipment?

Send your cargo description, dimensions, weight, origin, UK destination and preferred timeframe. Oakview Freight Services will review the requirements and discuss suitable options.
